### What is a bad design?

By bad design I mean a design without a structure. We don’t go into esthetics though I notice there is a strong corellation between a structured and esthetically pleasing design.

- Chisel the design. Don’t go into abstraction from the start.
- Choose a framework
- Include tolerance for values
- Don’t take “pixel-perfect” literally
- Keep an eye for patterns and room for abstractions
- Utility first styles
- Leave the specifics for the end
- Analyze the design first
